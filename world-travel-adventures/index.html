World Travel Adventures

World Travel Adventures

Run ventures in real cities, learn the stories behind them, collect passport stamps and dress your traveler for the trip. Start in Cape Town and work your way around the world.

▶ Play the preview

Opens in a new tab. Free, with no sign-up. It's about a 10 MB download and made for phones, though it runs on a computer too.

In the game

  • 44 real cities with their own ventures, local money and music
  • Facts, stories and quizzes that fill your passport with stamps
  • Phrasebooks for 22 countries
  • A traveler to dress, with thousands of items to hunt, craft and collect
  • The Trivia Tower, a quiz climb through the continents

This is an early preview

The game is still being built, so things will change. Most cities still have placeholder art, and we're still checking some facts and phrasebook words against more sources. Found a mistake? Please tell us.

Your progress saves in your browser on this device. The preview has no ads and nothing to buy, and the game doesn't collect anything about you.
