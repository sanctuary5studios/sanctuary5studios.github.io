About SFS

We believe technology should create meaningful possibilities.

Sanctuary Five Studios is a technology company. We help businesses, startups and individuals solve problems with software, AI and smarter systems, and we develop original digital products of our own.

Get in touch How we work
Our vision

Technology that moves ideas forward.

Good technology should make work simpler, open new opportunities and bring ideas to life. That belief guides everything we do.

We work across two complementary areas. Through our client services, we deliver software, AI and business systems solutions. Through SFS Interactive, we create our own games and digital products. Building our own products keeps us close to what makes technology useful and enjoyable, and that experience shapes the work we do for clients.

Our development philosophy

Principles behind our work.

We focus on solutions that make a real difference, not technology for its own sake.

Our approach

How we work.

A clear, collaborative process that we adapt to each project.

Stage 01 of 05

Discover

We learn about your goals, the people involved and what success looks like, so we focus on the right problem from the start.

Stage 02 of 05

Plan

We agree priorities, scope and a sensible way forward, so you know what to expect before work begins.

Stage 03 of 05

Develop

We build in focused stages and share progress along the way, so you can see things taking shape.

Stage 04 of 05

Refine

We test, gather feedback and make improvements until everything works as it should.

Stage 05 of 05

Deliver

We launch or roll out, guide your team through it, and talk through options for ongoing support.

Every project is different, so we adapt this approach to suit your needs.
