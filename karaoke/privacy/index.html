Sanctuary Five Studios Karaoke

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026
Publisher: Sanctuary Five Studios ("we", "us")
Contact: hello@sanctuaryfivestudios.com

This policy explains how the Sanctuary Five Studios Karaoke desktop application ("the App") handles information. In short: the App works entirely on your computer. It does not collect, send or sell any personal information, and it does not connect to the internet.

1. Information we collect

None. The App has no user accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting or automatic updates. It does not send any information to Sanctuary Five Studios or to anyone else.

The App also technically blocks all outgoing internet connections from inside the program. The only time an internet page is involved is when you click Buy full version, which opens the store page in your own web browser (see section 5).

2. Information stored on your computer

To work, the App saves the following only on your own computer, in its data folder (by default %APPDATA%\Sanctuary Five Studios Karaoke, or a location you choose in Settings):

InformationWhy it is stored
Your media library index: file locations, song titles, artists, durations, play counts, categories, notesTo list, search and play your songs. Your media files themselves are never changed, uploaded or copied elsewhere.
Singer names, song requests, rotation and performance history, preferred keysKaraoke hosting features
Playlists, queue, filler playlist, settings, saved layouts, lyric timing editsTo remember your setup between sessions
Song analysis (waveforms, tempo, musical key) and converted media cacheTo speed up playback and display
Licence details: the name or email and purchase key you enterTo confirm your licence. Keys are checked offline; nothing is sent to us.
Diagnostic log filesTo help you troubleshoot. The logs stay on your computer unless you choose to share them.
Automatic backups of the aboveTo protect against data loss

3. Microphone, audio devices and displays

If you choose a microphone, the App processes its sound live on your computer to mix it with the music. Microphone audio is not recorded or saved unless you press Record, and recordings are saved only to the location you choose. The App reads the names of your audio devices and displays only so you can select them.

4. Information about other people

If you host karaoke, you may type other people's names (singers) into the App. That information stays on your computer. As the host, you are responsible for handling it appropriately, for example by deleting singer history you no longer need (Settings → Storage & backup, or by removing singers).

5. Purchases

Purchases of the full version are processed by our online store or payment provider, not by the App. When you buy, that provider collects the information needed to complete the purchase (such as your name, email and payment details) under its own privacy policy. We receive what is needed to issue your purchase key (typically your name or email and order details) and use it only to provide, support and verify your licence, and to meet legal and accounting requirements.

6. Children

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Keeping, exporting and deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, you are in control:

  • Export: Settings → Storage & backup → Export settings, or copy the data folder.
  • Delete: uninstalling the App leaves your data in place so that reinstalling keeps your library. To delete it permanently, delete the data folder listed in Settings → Storage & backup.
  • Licence: About → Licence → Remove licence from this PC.

8. Security

Your data is protected by your computer's own account security. The App writes its files safely to avoid corruption and keeps rolling backups. It does not open network ports or accept connections from other devices.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK/EU GDPR, Canadian PIPEDA, Australian Privacy Act or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information an organisation holds about you. The App itself sends us nothing, so the only personal information we may hold is what you provided when purchasing or contacting support. Contact us at the address above to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled (for example by adding an optional online feature), we will update this policy and change the effective date. Any such feature would be off unless you turn it on.

11. Contact

Sanctuary Five Studios — hello@sanctuaryfivestudios.com — New Zealand

This policy covers the desktop app. For this website, see our website privacy notice.
