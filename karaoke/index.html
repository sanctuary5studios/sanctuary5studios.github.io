Sanctuary Five Studios Karaoke

A karaoke hosting, music and video player for hosts, parties and home use, with a separate audience screen for your TV or projector. It works fully offline, with no account, no subscription and no ads.

Made for hosts

Run the whole night from one screen.

Line up your singers, play their songs on two decks, keep music going between performances, and show lyrics and announcements on a separate TV or projector for the audience.

  • Fully offline. No account, no subscription, no internet connection, no ads and no telemetry. The app blocks internet access, so nothing leaves your PC.
  • Plays what you already have. MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC/M4A, OGG, AIFF, WMA, MP4, MKV, WEBM, MOV, AVI, MPEG, WMV and FLV. Files Windows can't play directly are converted automatically, and your original files are never modified.
  • A real audience screen. Lyrics, “Now singing” banners and announcements on any connected display.

Features

Everything a karaoke night needs.

Karaoke formats

  • MP3+G and WAV+G
  • ZIP MP3+G, with no unzipping needed
  • Karaoke videos
  • LRC synced lyrics, including word-by-word highlighting
  • Lyrics embedded in MP3 tags, synced and plain
  • Multiplex tracks with a selectable vocal channel and guide-vocal level

Audience display

  • Choose any connected TV, projector or monitor
  • Fullscreen and scaling
  • Black, colour, image or looping-video backgrounds
  • “Now singing” and “Up next” banners
  • Announcements, a scrolling ticker and a logo overlay
  • Blackout button, and automatic recovery if the TV is unplugged

Singer rotation

  • Fixed or automatic order
  • Several songs per singer
  • Key change per singer and song
  • Drag-and-drop reordering, skip, repeat and away
  • Performance history and remembered singer preferences
  • Song requests

Two decks and a mixer

  • Play, cue and hot cues with waveforms
  • BPM and key detection
  • Tempo with key lock, and key change in semitones
  • 3-band EQ, filters and crossfader
  • Master limiter and loudness levelling
  • Headphone preview on a second audio device

Microphone

  • Volume and EQ
  • Reverb and echo
  • Talk-over: the music ducks while you speak
  • Basic feedback reduction

Music, library and more

  • Background filler music between singers that fades out automatically
  • Music queue with Automix, and M3U playlists
  • Fast library with watched folders, external and network drives
  • Recording to MP3, WAV, FLAC or M4A
  • Four layouts: Karaoke, Music, Video and Advanced
  • Themes, configurable shortcuts, automatic backups and crash recovery

System requirements

What you need.

Required

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • About 118 MB for the installer, plus space for your library and cache
  • Your own songs, karaoke tracks or videos
  • No internet connection needed

Optional

  • A second display (TV, projector or monitor) for the audience screen
  • A microphone
  • A second audio device for headphone preview

Audio uses standard Windows audio (WASAPI). ASIO isn't supported.

Questions

Good to know.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. It works fully offline, with no account, no subscription, no ads and no telemetry. The app actively blocks internet access, and nothing leaves your PC.

What happens when the demo ends?

After 10 minutes, playback fades and a purchase screen appears. You can restart the demo for another 10 minutes. The demo allows up to 3 singers, doesn't record, and shows an “UNLICENSED DEMO” watermark on the audience screen.

How much is the full version?

NZ$79, as a one-off payment with no subscription. Purchasing opens soon; until then, the free demo lets you try everything.

How do I unlock the full version?

After you buy, we email you a purchase key. In the app, open ☰ → “Enter purchase key…” (or use the demo screen) and enter your name or email exactly as on your purchase, plus the key. Keys are checked offline.

Does it come with songs?

No. The app doesn't include any songs or lyrics. You use your own media, and you're responsible for any performance licences needed at public events.

Which file types does it play?

Karaoke: MP3+G, WAV+G, ZIP MP3+G, karaoke videos, LRC synced lyrics and lyrics embedded in MP3 tags. Audio and video: MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC/M4A, OGG, AIFF, WMA, MP4, MKV, WEBM, MOV, AVI, MPEG, WMV and FLV. Files Windows can't play directly are converted automatically, and your originals are never modified.

Can it remove vocals?

Multiplex karaoke tracks have a selectable vocal channel and a guide-vocal level. For other recordings, vocal reduction is basic, and how well it works depends on the recording.

Does it support ASIO?

No. It uses standard Windows audio (WASAPI).

Is there a Mac version?

Not at the moment. The app runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit).

Why does Windows show a warning when I install it?

Our installer is new to Microsoft SmartScreen, so Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info → Run anyway.
