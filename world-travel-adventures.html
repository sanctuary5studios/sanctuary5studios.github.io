World Travel Adventures

Run businesses in real cities, learn about the world, and fly your traveler around the globe.

World Travel Adventures is a cozy educational idle game. You start in Cape Town running local ventures like a koesister kitchen and penguin tours, hire local guides to keep them going, and earn medals as each city grows.

Every milestone unlocks a short fact, fun stat or story about the place, stamped into your passport. Then you pack up and fly to a new mystery destination, with 44 real cities across 22 countries to discover.

How it plays

Grow a city, learn its story, fly on.

Run local ventures

Start in Cape Town with ventures like a koesister kitchen and penguin tours, and hire local guides to keep them going.

Learn as you grow

Earn medals as each city grows. Every milestone unlocks a fact, fun stat or story, stamped into your passport.

Fly somewhere new

Pack up and fly to a mystery destination. There are 44 real cities across 22 countries to discover.

Highlights

Plenty to discover.

44 real cities, 22 countries

Each city has its own ventures, local money and hand-picked facts and stories.

Passport stamps and quizzes

Collect stamps as you go, then put what you've learned to the test with quizzes and the Trivia Tower.

Phrasebooks for 22 countries

Learn everyday words like Molo (isiXhosa) and Konnichiwa (Japanese).

Dress up your traveler

A traveler and companion to style, with more than 2,600 collectible outfits, accessories and pets.

Travel cases and a workshop

Open travel cases (the odds are always shown), and craft and upgrade items in the workshop.

A theme for every country

An original music theme for each country you visit.
