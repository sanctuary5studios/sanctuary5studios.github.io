How the game works

Snuggle Sanctuary is a cosy idle/clicker game. You build up an animal sanctuary, watch it earn coins on its own, and use those coins to grow it further. There's no way to "lose" — it's about steady, relaxing progress.

🐾The core loop

You start with a founding pair of animals. Pat them for hearts, and over time new animals arrive on their own. Each animal earns coins automatically, faster the more you've upgraded its care. Coins buy care upgrades and facilities, which raise your income, which lets you afford the next thing.

🏡Zones and animals

20 animals are split across 4 zones (5 each), unlocked in order as you progress. Each animal can also be rehomed — sent off to a good home for an adoption fee — after which the space can fill again. The founding pair in each zone always stays.

📈Care upgrades

Each animal has 4 upgrade tracks (arrivals, staying close, adoption fee, space) up to level 20. Levelling these is the main way you spend coins early on.

🌱Rewilding (prestige)

Once your sanctuary is well developed, you can rewild — release everything and start the zone over — in exchange for permanent conservation stars that boost your income forever after.

🛡️Guardians

8 rare guardian animals can be unlocked with conservation stars, each giving a strong permanent bonus. Slots are limited, so which guardians you pick matters.

🎪Starlight Fair

A bonus world you unlock through play (or purchase) containing an outfit wardrobe, and two mini-games — a match-3 and a lane-dodging game — played with tickets that refill over time.

📖The story

A tap-through comic runs alongside your progress, following your ranger caring for Nana's old sanctuary. It unlocks in chapters as you play — no need to seek it out.

What to test

Work through these roughly in order — they follow how a new player would naturally progress. Times are a rough guide, not a target.

🚪

First launch & onboarding

Set up your ranger (gender, skin tone, username), play through the intro comic and locked tutorial. Note anything confusing, stuck, or skippable that shouldn't be.

5 min
🐣

Core loop & first zone

Pat animals, watch arrivals happen, rehome a couple, and push care upgrades on at least one animal to a high level. Confirm the egg-hatch animation plays on a new species.

10 min
🏗️

Facilities, totems & partner sanctuaries

Buy a facility, complete a zone totem, and check a partner sanctuary. Confirm income actually goes up after each purchase.

8 min
🌱

Your first rewild

Do at least one full rewild. Check your conservation stars land correctly and your permanent bonus is visibly applied afterward.

5 min
🎁

Ads & VIP

Watch a couple of optional (rewarded) ads. Confirm they're always skippable, the reward matches what was promised, and your kindness jar / VIP progress updates.

5 min
🎪

Starlight Fair

Once unlocked: try on a wardrobe item, play both mini-games at least once, and use a ticket refill. Check ticket gifting if you're in a herd.

10 min
🌐

Cloud save & herds

Sign in with Google, force-close and reopen the app to confirm your save restored. If you can, test on a second device or with a friend's herd code.

10 min
⚙️

Settings & general feel

Try Day/Night/Follow-phone themes, battery saver mode, and sound sets. Note anything that feels slow, cluttered, or pushes you to spend money.

5 min
4

How to report issues

For anything that breaks or feels off, send:

  1. What you were doing right before it happened
  2. What you expected vs. what actually happened
  3. Your device model and Android version
  4. A screenshot or screen recording if you can grab one
