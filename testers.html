🧪 Closed testing · Android

Be one of the first to visit the sanctuary.

We're looking for about 25 Android players to try Snuggle Sanctuary for roughly two weeks and tell us honestly how it feels.

✉️ Sign up by email💬 Join the Discord
  • Free
  • A few minutes a day
  • No experience needed

How it works

  1. Send us a quick email

    Tap the sign-up button above. It opens an email with a few short questions already filled in, so just answer and send. It takes about two minutes.

  2. We add you to the test

    We add the Gmail address you use on Google Play to the tester list and reply with confirmation, as soon as we can.

  3. Opt in and install

    Open the opt-in link on your Android phone (it works once we've added you), join the test, then install from Google Play.

  4. Play for about two weeks

    Drop in when you like, a few minutes a day is plenty. Google needs testers to stay opted in for 14 days, so please don't leave the test early.

  5. Tell us what you think

    Send feedback through Google Play at least twice, or share it in our Discord. Bugs, confusing bits, things you loved, all welcome.

What we ask of you

  • An Android phone and a Gmail address
  • Play on most days for about 2 weeks
  • Send feedback at least twice
  • Stay opted in for the full 14 days
  • Be 13 or older

A thank-you from us 🎁

  • You help shape the game and may see your ideas in it
  • A thank-you gift code for in-game goodies after launch
  • The gift is for taking part, never for positive feedback or reviews

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Testing is completely free.

What will I be playing?

An idle sanctuary game: you care for animal families, they grow on their own, and you can rehome them to loving homes. There's also a short comic story. See the screenshots on the home page.

Will my progress carry over to launch?

No. We reset test progress before the public launch so everyone starts fresh. Test builds may also change or have bugs, which is exactly what we're here to find.

Does the game have ads or purchases?

Ads are optional and rewarded: you only see one if you choose to. During testing, ads and purchases run in test mode, so you won't be charged anything and an ad may sometimes not be available.

What data do you collect?

See our privacy policy. You can also ask us to delete your data any time via the account deletion page.

What if I find a bug?

Great, that's the point! Send it through Google Play feedback or post in the Discord with your phone model and what you were doing. A screenshot helps a lot.

Can I leave the test?

Of course. We'd just ask you to stay for the full 14 days if you can, because Google counts opted-in days toward our release requirements.

Ready to join?

Send us a quick email and we'll take it from there.
