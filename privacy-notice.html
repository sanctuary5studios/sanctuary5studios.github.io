Website privacy notice

How we handle information you share with us through this website. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

This notice covers sanctuaryfivestudios.com and the enquiries you send us through it. Our games have their own policy: see the Snuggle Sanctuary Privacy Policy.

Who we are

Sanctuary Five Studios ("SFS", "we", "us") is a technology company based in New Zealand. You can contact us at hello@sanctuaryfivestudios.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through a form on this website, or email us, we collect the details you choose to provide. These can include:

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number and organisation, if you provide them
  • your preferred way of being contacted and suitable times
  • the details of your enquiry and any answers you give in the solution finder

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, arrange any call or meeting you ask for, and, if you'd like to work with us, discuss and prepare a proposal. We won't add you to marketing lists unless you ask us to.

How your enquiry reaches us

Depending on how this website is set up, a form either sends your enquiry to us through a form-processing service that delivers it to our business email, or opens your own email app so you can send it yourself. Either way, your enquiry is stored in our business email account. Service providers we use to receive email or form submissions may process your information on our behalf, and may store it outside New Zealand.

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone else for their own purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If you switch between dark and light mode, your browser remembers that choice on your device; it isn't sent to us. Our web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and our website host keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run the service securely.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as we need it to respond and for any ongoing relationship that follows, and then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you can ask to access the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Contact us at hello@sanctuaryfivestudios.com. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

Changes

We'll update this notice if the way we handle information changes, and update the date above.
