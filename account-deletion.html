Snuggle Sanctuary

Delete Your Account

Last updated: 8 October 2026

You can delete your Snuggle Sanctuary online account and its data at any time. Snuggle Sanctuary is published by Sanctuary Five Studios.

Who this applies to

Everyone who plays online. The game keeps a cloud backup for every player, including guests who never link a Google account, so this page applies whether or not you've signed in with Google.

Option 1: delete it in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Snuggle Sanctuary and tap the ⚙️ Settings button.
  2. Tap Online account and cloud save.
  3. Tap Delete my online account and confirm.

This happens straight away. Your sanctuary on that phone stays, and it starts backing up to a new guest account. To remove the copy on your phone as well, use Settings → Delete all my progress or uninstall the game.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you no longer have the game installed, email us with the subject line "Delete my account":

✉️ sanctuary5studios@gmail.com

To help us find your account, please include:

  1. Your ranger name (every ranger name is one of a kind)
  2. The Google account email you linked, if you linked one
  3. Your herd code, if you're in a herd

We process email requests by hand and aim to finish within 30 days. We'll confirm by email when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (the random user ID and, if linked, the connection to your Google account)
  • Your cloud backup (animals, currencies, upgrades, outfits, story and purchases you own)
  • Your ranger name record, so the name becomes free for others
  • Your leaderboard and event entries, and any event prizes
  • Your herd membership, help points and ticket gifts
  • Your notification token and notification choices
Deleting your online account can't be undone. Items bought with real money can be restored from Google Play with the same Google account.

What we may keep

World totals that already include your contribution (such as the Great Migration and weekly vote counts) stay as anonymous totals that don't identify you. Google Play keeps its own purchase records under its own policies; we never store your payment details.

Questions

If you have any questions about this process or your data, contact us:

✉️ sanctuary5studios@gmail.com

See also our Privacy Policy for what the game collects and why.
