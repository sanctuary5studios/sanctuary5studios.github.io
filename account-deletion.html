Snuggle Sanctuary

Delete Your Account

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Snuggle Sanctuary account and the data associated with it, published by Sanctuary Five Studios.

Who this applies to

Snuggle Sanctuary can be played as a guest, with no account required. This page applies if you've linked a Google account for cloud save, herds, or other online features, and want that account and its associated data removed.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address you'd like us to reply to, with the subject line "Delete my account":

✉️ sanctuary5studios@gmail.com

To help us find your account, please include:

  1. Your in-game username (shown in Settings → Online account)
  2. The Google account email you linked, if you know it
  3. Your herd code, if you're part of a herd

What gets deleted

Once we verify your request, we delete:

  • Your linked Google account identifier and email association
  • Your cloud-saved game progress (sanctuary state, animals, currencies, upgrades)
  • Your in-game username and herd membership
  • Any ticket-gifting or herd-related records tied to your account
Deleting your account removes your cloud save entirely. If you're also using guest/local play on a device, that device's local save is separate and isn't affected by this request — you'd need to uninstall the app to clear that.

What we may keep

We may retain anonymised or aggregated data that no longer identifies you (for example, general usage statistics) after your account is deleted. Purchase records that Google Play itself retains for tax, accounting, or legal purposes are governed by Google Play's own policies, not ours — we don't store your payment details ourselves.

How long it takes

As a small independent studio, we currently process deletion requests by hand. We aim to complete deletions within 30 days of a verified request, and will confirm by email once it's done.

Questions

If you have any questions about this process or your data, contact us:

✉️ sanctuary5studios@gmail.com

See also our Privacy Policy for more on what data we collect and why.
