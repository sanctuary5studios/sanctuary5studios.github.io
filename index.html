Snuggle Sanctuary

🐣🌸

"Your sanctuary is waiting for you."

A quiet place to care for someone small

Create your own ranger and step into a sanctuary passed down with love. Help twenty adorable animal families settle in across four cosy zones. Watch them arrive, grow, and — when they're ready — find loving homes of their own.

  • 🧑‍🌾 Create your own ranger — choose their look and give them a name
  • 🐾 Twenty animal families across four zones, each with its own personality and animation
  • 💛 A gentle idle economy — hearts for everyday care, rare gems for special moments
  • 📖 A tap-through story about love, rehoming, and letting go
  • 🌤️ Day, night and battery-saver looks, with soft generated music

Built to feel kind

Ethical by design

Ads are always optional and rewarded — never interruptions, never required to progress. You can enjoy the whole sanctuary without spending a cent.

🎨

Handmade kawaii art

Every animal is drawn in a soft, dimensional "mochi" style with its own unique animation — original characters, original world.

A story with heart

Beneath the idle-game loop is a comic about your ranger, a sanctuary's legacy, and what it means to let go with love.
