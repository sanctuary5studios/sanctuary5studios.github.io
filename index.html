What it's like

🐾

Twenty animal families

Across four cozy zones, each with its own personality and its own animation. Families grow on their own while you're away.

🏡

Rehome with love

When animals are ready, help them find loving homes of their own. Sharing love is the heart of the game.

📖

A story with heart

A tap-through comic about Kiri the kiwi, who inherits her late Nana's sanctuary, and what it means to love with open hands.

✨

Gentle by design

Ads are always optional and rewarded, never interruptions. You can enjoy the whole sanctuary without spending a cent.

Want to help us get ready for launch?
