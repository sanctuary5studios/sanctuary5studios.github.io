A realistic view

AI is a powerful assistant, not a magic switch.

AI can draft, summarise, organise and analyse remarkably well. It still needs clear direction and a human eye for accuracy and judgement.

That's why we focus on practical skills and sensible use: helping you spend less time on routine work and more on what matters, without overpromising what AI can do.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need technical skills?

No. We start from wherever you are and keep things practical and easy to follow.

Is this for individuals or businesses?

Both. We work with individuals one-to-one and with teams and organisations.

Which AI tools do you recommend?

We aren't tied to any single provider. We suggest tools that fit your needs, budget and data requirements.

Can AI replace our staff or processes?

That's rarely the right goal. AI works best supporting people with specific tasks. We help you find those realistic opportunities.

How are sessions delivered?

Typically online by video call, at times that suit you. Other arrangements can be discussed.

How much does it cost?

Every engagement is priced individually. Get in touch and we'll put together a tailored quote.
