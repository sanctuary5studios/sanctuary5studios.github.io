Snuggle Sanctuary

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Sanctuary Five Studios ("we", "us", "our") built the game Snuggle Sanctuary as a free, ad- and purchase-supported app. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

Snuggle Sanctuary can be played entirely as a guest, with no account required. If you choose to link a Google account for cloud save, herds, or other online features, we collect and store the following through Firebase (Google's app development platform):

  • Account info — your Google account email address and a unique user ID, used to identify your account and sign you back in on other devices.
  • Game data — your in-game username, sanctuary progress (animals, currencies, upgrades), herd membership, and related records, so your progress can sync and be restored.

If you play as a guest without linking an account, we don't collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying information through the app itself — your progress stays on your device.

The app may also collect the following, through third-party services described below:

  • Advertising data — when you choose to watch a rewarded ad, our ad partner (Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Purchase data — if you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Google Play's billing system. We do not see or store your payment details (card numbers, etc.) — these are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Basic app diagnostics — general crash and performance data may be collected automatically by Google Play services to help us fix bugs.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Snuggle Sanctuary uses optional, rewarded video ads only — never full-screen interstitials or ads required to progress. Ads are served through Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to personalize ads or measure ad performance, depending on your device and consent settings. You can control ad personalization through your device's settings, and where required, we present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before showing personalized ads.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see Google's Partner Sites policy.

In-app purchases

Snuggle Sanctuary offers optional in-app purchases (such as heart or gem packs) processed through Google Play's billing system. All payment information is handled by Google Play directly under its own privacy and security policies — we do not receive or store your payment card details.

Children's privacy

Snuggle Sanctuary is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed as a children's app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete it.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected by our third-party service providers (Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play billing, Google Play services) is subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase data disclosure
  • AdMob data disclosure

Data retention and deletion

If you play as a guest, your in-game progress is stored locally on your device, and uninstalling the app removes this data. If you link a Google account, your progress and account info are also stored in the cloud via Firebase so they can sync across devices. You can request deletion of your account and its associated cloud data at any time — see our Account Deletion page for how.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads, on most Android devices).
  • You can decline the ad-consent prompt where shown; this may result in seeing non-personalized ads instead.
  • You can play as a guest to avoid creating an account, and uninstall the app at any time to remove locally stored data.
  • You can request deletion of your linked account and cloud-saved data — see Account Deletion.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example as we add new features. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
