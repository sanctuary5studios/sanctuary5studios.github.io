The game

What it's like to play.

Twenty animal families

Across four cozy zones, each with its own personality and animation. Families grow on their own while you're away.

Rehome with love

When animals are ready, help them find loving homes of their own. Sharing love is the heart of the game.

A story with heart

A tap-through comic about you, a ranger who inherits your late Nana's sanctuary.

Gentle by design

Ads are always optional and rewarded, never interruptions. Enjoy the whole sanctuary without spending a cent.
