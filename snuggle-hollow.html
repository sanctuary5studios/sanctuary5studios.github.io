Snuggle Hollow

Care for cozy animals, and they'll lend a paw.

Snuggle Hollow is a cozy 3D life-and-crafting adventure for phones, set in a quiet valley that's still recovering from a long flood. You arrive with Clover the bunny at your side, gather wood and stone, fix up the little town and make friends with the people who live there.

Along the way you take in animals from the Snuggle Sanctuary family and look after them yourself: feeding them, carrying water from the well and giving them a pat. As their trust grows, they start helping out around the Hollow.
