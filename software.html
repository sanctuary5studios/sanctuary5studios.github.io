Questions

Good to know.

I only have an idea. Is that enough?

Yes. Many good products start as a conversation. We can help you shape the idea into something buildable, often starting with a prototype.

Which technologies do you use?

It depends on your project. We recommend an approach once we understand your requirements, existing systems and budget.

How long does a project take?

Every project is different. After an initial discussion we can outline a realistic plan for your situation.

Can you improve software we already have?

Often, yes. We're happy to look at existing software and talk through what could be improved.

How much does it cost?

Every project is priced individually. Tell us what you need and we'll provide a tailored quote after we've discussed it.

Got an idea worth building?

Tell us about it. We'll help you work out a smart way to bring it to life.

A free 15-minute discovery call is available on request, subject to availability.
